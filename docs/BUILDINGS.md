## 4. Production & Crafting

**Owner layout rules (2026-08-24):**
- Crafter shops (pottery, weaver, tannery, cobbler, tailor) are **4×2 tiles** — a 2×2 dwelling plus a 2×2 roofless open-air workshop (rear work frame) with the craft's tools and a sales stall facing the long (south) edge. Their roofs are straw, turning to clay tile when the workshop is upgraded (toolmaker too).
- Forester, herbalist, hunter, fisher, woodcutter are also **4×2**: hut + fenced sapling garden / herb garden with cauldron & table / fenced skinning yard / plank dock / fenced log yard with axe & stump. **The fisher's dock side must reach open water to place** (R rotates which side the dock is on).
- All plain houses wear straw roofs; every doorway has an explicit closed door; dwellings stand on a material-matched platform (planks for wood, stone for stone/brick) with everyday clutter (benches, stools, hay, barrels, firewood).
- Buildings rotate with **R** during placement; the ghost is the real building, semi-transparent.
- Dwellings sit 20% smaller in their plot with random props around them; chimneys smoke when the home has firewood outside summer.
